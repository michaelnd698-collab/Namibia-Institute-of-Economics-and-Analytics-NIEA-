WELCOME TO NIEA

Where learning meets
real-world application.

The Namibia Institute of Economics and Analytics (NIEA) is an independent professional education, research and analytical services organisation.

We bridge the gap between theory and practice through specialised training, hands-on projects and services that help people and organisations make better use of economic knowledge and data.
