GET TO KNOW US

Learning with purpose.

The Namibia Institute of Economics and Analytics (NIEA) is an independent professional education, research and analytical services organisation.

We aim to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and practical application through specialised training, research, data analytics and professional services.
